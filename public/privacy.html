Vigil — Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft — replace this date when reviewed and published.

Vigil is a personal recovery and accountability app. The data it collects is sensitive by nature, so this policy is written to be specific about what is collected, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

Account info (email, display name), urge/check-in logs (timestamp, outcome, optional trigger note), journal entries and mood selections, your PREP plan (warning signs, triggers, reasons, commitments, "why"), Moment and Recovery session data, and accountability-partner links you create.

Who can see it

Only you, by default. If you invite an accountability partner and they accept, they can see your urge logs, streak, and journal entries — never your PREP plan, which stays private to you. We never sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push subscription (endpoint and keys) used only to deliver your own reminders and, if applicable, partner alerts. You can disable this at any time in Settings or your device's notification permissions.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact the app owner directly.

Security

Data is stored with row-level security so that only you (and, where you've explicitly granted it, your active accountability partner) can read it.
